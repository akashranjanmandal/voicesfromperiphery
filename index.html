What is Voices from the Periphery?

Voices from the Periphery is a scholarly and archival platform devoted to the study and critical interpretation of subaltern histories, marginal communities, and alternative knowledge systems. Bringing together historical research, oral archives, interdisciplinary scholarship, and public intellectual engagement, the platform foregrounds voices historically excluded from dominant narratives, with a primary focus on South Asia while remaining attentive to comparative and transnational perspectives. Committed to methodological rigour and ethical research practices, Voices from the Periphery seeks to foster responsible archival work, critical dialogue, and sustained scholarly intervention in the study of marginality as both a historical condition and an analytical framework.

Our Mission

The mission of Voices from the Periphery is to advance interdisciplinary research and public scholarship on marginality through publications, oral archives, lectures, and collaborative fieldwork. With a primary focus on South Asia and an openness to comparative and transnational perspectives, the platform brings together scholars, researchers, and practitioners to produce and disseminate historically grounded, methodologically sound, and socially engaged scholarship.

Our Vision

Voices from the Periphery envisions an intellectually rigorous and ethically grounded scholarly space that foregrounds subaltern histories and marginal voices while critically interrogating dominant historical narratives. The platform seeks to contribute to global debates on power, representation, and historical agency by treating the periphery not merely as a site of exclusion, but as a productive analytical framework for rethinking history, society, and knowledge.

Research Ethics

Voices from the Periphery is committed to ethical research practices that prioritise informed consent, responsible archival methods, and respectful representation of communities whose histories have been structurally marginalised. The platform upholds principles of academic integrity, reflexivity, and accountability, recognising the ethical responsibilities involved in documenting, interpreting, and circulating narratives from the margins.
