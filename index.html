Why Voices “from” the Periphery?

Why from—and not of?

Because we do not want to speak of the periphery as an object of study, nor claim to speak for those at its margins. We believe histories, memories, and knowledge can emerge from the periphery—shaping, questioning, and sometimes unsettling what we call the centre.

“From” is therefore a position, not a boundary: a way of listening to histories where they speak from.

02

Our Mission

The mission of Voices from the Periphery is to advance interdisciplinary research and public scholarship on marginality through publications, oral archives, lectures, and collaborative fieldwork. With a primary focus on South Asia and an openness to comparative and transnational perspectives, the platform brings together scholars, researchers, and practitioners to produce and disseminate historically grounded, methodologically sound, and socially engaged scholarship.

i

Our Vision

Voices from the Periphery envisions an intellectually rigorous and ethically grounded scholarly space that foregrounds subaltern histories and marginal voices while critically interrogating dominant historical narratives. The platform seeks to contribute to global debates on power, representation, and historical agency by treating the periphery not merely as a site of exclusion, but as a productive analytical framework for rethinking history, society, and knowledge.
