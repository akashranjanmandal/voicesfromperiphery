Case Studies

At Voices from the Periphery, we conduct case studies to understand the present as it is lived. We go to the field, engage with people and communities, gather information, and document contemporary social and political realities through direct observation and academic inquiry.

Our case studies bring together field research, public voices, evidence, and critical analysis to examine questions that matter in the present.

We go beyond observation—to the ground, to the people, to the story.
