The Tales of the Marginals

ISSN 3107 - 4995

The Tales of the Marginalss is a journal dedicated to amplifying the voices and narratives of those often silenced or overlooked by mainstream historical discourse. It delves into the lived experiences of marginalized communities, weaving together their histories, cultures, and perspectives to foster a more inclusive understanding of the past.
Committed to original and rigorous scholarship, the journal upholds the highest research standards, ensuring that each contribution is not only compelling but also methodologically sound. By documenting these narratives, we seek to honor the resilience and strength of those pushed to the periphery, ensuring their stories are told with the dignity and depth they deserve.

More About The Journal

+

In an era where dominant historical narratives continue to overshadow the voices of the marginalized, The Tales of the Marginalss serves as a critical platform for alternative perspectives that challenge conventional historiography. By centering the experiences of those who have long been relegated to the periphery, this journal seeks to reshape historical discourse and provide a space where subaltern voices are not just acknowledged but actively engaged with. Each issue will feature meticulously researched articles, case studies, and archival explorations that bring to light the untold stories of resistance, survival, and cultural transformation.
Beyond academic scholarship, The Tales of the Marginalss aspires to bridge the gap between history and contemporary social realities. By fostering interdisciplinary dialogue, the journal encourages contributions that connect historical inquiry with pressing issues of inequality, discrimination, and identity politics. Through this effort, we aim to cultivate a deeper understanding of the past while inspiring new approaches to justice, representation, and historical consciousness in the present.

The publisher of the journal is Voices from the Periphery.

What is the primary language of the journal or the paper?

The primary language of the journal is English, ensuring accessibility to a global academic audience.
