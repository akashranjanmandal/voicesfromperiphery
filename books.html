About

Our book series and online lecture archive on Subaltern Studies seek to re-narrate history from below—through the lives, resistances, and worldviews of those long excluded from dominant narratives. The series will bring together critical essays, archival discoveries, oral histories, and theoretical reflections, drawing on feminist, decolonial, and postcolonial perspectives, in monographs and edited volumes that are rigorous yet accessible.

Alongside it, our lecture series offers an open platform where scholars, practitioners, and community voices engage with questions of marginality, identity, and historical justice. Together, they reflect our commitment to write history with, not for, the marginalized.

Aayush Dey Editorial Director
