Who are we ?

Welcome to Voices from the Periphery, a distinguished forum dedicated to advancing scholarly discourse on Subaltern History. Our group is committed to fostering critical dialogue and intellectual exchange, centering on the narratives and lived experiences of marginalized communities. Through rigorous research and thoughtful engagement, we seek to illuminate voices often overlooked in mainstream historical narratives. In pursuit of this mission, we publish a biannual academic journal that explores pressing issues in the field, offering fresh perspectives and incisive analyses to enrich contemporary historical scholarship.

Know more about us

+

At Voices from the Periphery, we are dedicated to bridging the gap between academic research and real-world social challenges by fostering a dynamic platform for scholars, students, and practitioners. Our mission is to facilitate meaningful engagement with Subaltern histories, ensuring that the narratives of marginalized communities are not only studied but also actively integrated into broader historical discussions that impact both academia and society.
We believe that understanding history requires a multidimensional approach that moves beyond traditional academic silos. By encouraging interdisciplinary perspectives—drawing from history, sociology, anthropology, political science, literature, and more—we aim to cultivate fresh insights that challenge dominant narratives. We welcome diverse viewpoints, recognizing that a plurality of voices enriches scholarly debates and deepens our collective understanding of the past.
Through our biannual academic journal, public discussions, and research initiatives, we strive to create a space where intellectual inquiry meets real-world advocacy. Our goal is not only to advance historical scholarship but also to foster a more inclusive and equitable understanding of history—one that acknowledges and amplifies the voices of those who have long been excluded from mainstream historical narratives. By doing so, we hope to inspire critical thought, inform contemporary debates, and contribute to a more just and historically conscious society.
